1. SCOPE OF WORK

The Developer agrees to design, develop, and deploy a business website strictly in accordance with the specifications outlined in Annexure A (the "Scope Sheet"). The approved demo template at [Demo URL] serves as the definitive design and functional reference. Any work, features, or pages requested by the Client that fall outside the explicit boundaries of Annexure A shall be considered out-of-scope and will be billed separately at the Developer’s prevailing rates.

2. FINANCIAL TERMS

The total one-time fee for the services outlined in Annexure A is ₹6,000 (Indian Rupees Six Thousand Only), broken down as follows:

  • Custom Business Website Development: ₹5,000
  • No-Code Owner Admin Application (Content Editing): ₹1,000
  • Note: This fee is exclusive of any applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) or other statutory levies, which shall be borne by the Client.

3. PAYMENT SCHEDULE

  • Advance Payment: 50% (₹3,000) is due via UPI prior to the commencement of any work. This advance is strictly non-refundable once development has begun, as it covers immediate labor allocation, infrastructure setup, and domain procurement.
  • Final Payment: The remaining 50% (₹3,000) is due in full upon project completion, prior to final handover, credential transfer, or domain go-live.
  • No exceptions. The Developer retains the right to withhold delivery until the final invoice is cleared.

4. TIMELINE & DEPENDENCIES

The estimated timeline for completion is 7 working days, commencing only after: (a) receipt of the advance payment, and (b) receipt of all required Client content (text, logos, images, pricing).

  • Client Delays: Any delay by the Client in providing content, feedback, or approvals will result in a day-for-day extension of the project timeline. The Developer is not liable for missed deadlines caused by Client inaction.

5. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS & WARRANTIES

The Client agrees to:

  1. Provide accurate, complete, and timely content.
  2. Warrant that they own or have the legal right to use all photos, logos, and text provided to the Developer, and will indemnify the Developer against any third-party copyright or intellectual property claims arising from Client-provided materials.
  3. Provide consolidated, actionable feedback within 3 working days of each review milestone.

6. REVISIONS & SCOPE CREEP

  • The project includes 2 rounds of revisions limited to the approved design reference in Annexure A.
  • Additional revision rounds will be billed at ₹300 per round.
  • Requests for new pages, features, or structural changes constitute a Change Order and will be quoted and billed separately.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (THE "BLUEPRINT" RULE)

  • Client Ownership: Upon full and final payment, the Client is granted ownership of the specific website instance, final content, and uploaded media.
  • Developer Retention: The Developer retains all right, title, and interest in and to all pre-existing code, reusable templates, frameworks, no-code architectures, tools, and proprietary know-how used to build the site. The Developer reserves the right to reuse these foundational elements for other clients.
  • Portfolio Rights: The Developer retains the right to display the finished website in its public portfolio and marketing materials, unless the Client explicitly objects in writing prior to launch.

8. DOMAIN & HOSTING INFRASTRUCTURE

  • The domain name shall be registered in the Client’s name.
  • First-Year Hosting: Included in the project fee.
  • Subsequent Years: Renewal for domain, hosting, and basic maintenance is ₹1,500/year, payable annually in advance.
  • Non-Renewal: If the renewal fee is not paid, the site will be granted a 15-day grace period. Thereafter, the Developer reserves the right to suspend services and take the site offline. The Client may request a one-time export of their content prior to suspension.

9. ADMIN APPLICATION & TRAINING

The fee includes one (1) training session (conducted in-person or via WhatsApp video call). This training is strictly limited to demonstrating how to edit existing content (prices, offers, photos, text) via the provided no-code admin interface. Training on new functionality or custom development is not included.

10. POST-LAUNCH SUPPORT

The Developer provides 30 days of complimentary support from the date of launch, strictly limited to fixing "Bugs."

  • Definition of a Bug: A verifiable failure of the website to function as explicitly specified in Annexure A.
  • Exclusions: Bug fixes do not include new feature development, content entry, design tweaks, or issues arising from the Client’s unauthorized modification of the site or third-party platform updates.

11. NO GUARANTEES OF BUSINESS RESULTS

The Developer is an engineering partner, not a marketing agency. While the website will be built with basic on-page SEO foundations, the Developer makes no guarantees regarding search engine rankings, website traffic, lead generation, or sales revenue. These outcomes are dependent on external market forces, search engine algorithms, and the Client’s business operations.

12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, the Developer’s total aggregate liability for any claims arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be strictly capped at the total fees actually paid by the Client to the Developer under this Agreement. The Developer shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including loss of profits or business interruption.

13. TERMINATION

Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing written notice.

  • If the Client terminates after work has commenced, the advance payment is forfeited as liquidated damages for allocated resources, and the Developer will hand over whatever work has been completed up to that date.
  • If the Developer terminates due to Client breach (e.g., non-payment, failure to provide content), the Client remains liable for all work completed up to the termination date.

14. GOVERNING LAW, JURISDICTION & ACCEPTANCE

  • Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India, including the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
  • Dispute Resolution: The Parties agree to first attempt to resolve any dispute amicably through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 15 days, the dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Rupnagar, Punjab.
  • Digital Acceptance: Pursuant to the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Indian Evidence Act, 1872, explicit approval of this Agreement, Annexure A, or project milestones via WhatsApp message or email from the Client’s registered phone number or email address shall constitute legally binding acceptance and execution of this contract.
